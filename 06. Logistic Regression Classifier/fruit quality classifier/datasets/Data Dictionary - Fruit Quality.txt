Data Dictionary - Fruit Dataset

A_id is the unique identifier for the sample in the dataset
Size is the size of the fruit
Weight is the weight of the fruit
Sweetness is the sweetness level of the fruit
Crunchiness is the crunchiness level of the fruit
Juiciness is the juiciness level of the fruit
Ripeness is the ripeness level of the fruit
Acidity is the fruit's acidity level
Quality is the fruit's quality, which can be good or bad

All numerical values in the dataset (except A_id) have been pre-transformed using a StandardScaler
