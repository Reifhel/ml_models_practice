# Instructions

###### _Project Structure, Rules, and Requirements_

In this challenge, you will act as a data analyst at a sales company, with the task of forecasting the revenue generated by salespeople based on information such as years of experience, number of sales, and seasonal factors. The goal is to apply regression techniques to analyze the impact of these variables and identify the most effective model for revenue forecasting.

Follow the suggested workflow to load, explore, model, and compare the results, always documenting your decisions and analyses. At the end, choose one of the options to make the trained model available, based on your preference and experience.

### Dataset:

`time_of_experience:` Time in months that the salesperson has worked at the company.

`number_of_sales:` Number of sales made by the salesperson in a specific period.

`seasonal_factor:` A factor (from 1 to 10) representing the seasonality of sales (e.g., 10 represents peak season).

`revenue_in_reais:` Total revenue generated by the salesperson.

### Deploying the Model

As we saw in the previous modules, there are different ways to deploy a trained model to predict data. Choose between an API, an app with Gradio, or an application that uses the API and front-end access via Streamlit.

Below you will find some important steps you must follow to ensure the challenge meets the requirements!

## Tasks

Use this checklist to help organize your delivery

- [ ] Load the sales_data.csv dataset using Pandas.
- [ ] Conduct an exploratory analysis of the data to identify correlations between the variables.
- [ ] Divide the data into training and test sets.
- [ ] Train a linear regression model to predict revenue based on length of service and number of sales.
- [ ] Evaluate the performance of the linear model using metrics such as MSE and R².
- [ ] Train a polynomial regression model, including the seasonal factor as an additional variable.
- [ ] Evaluate the performance of the polynomial model and compare it with the linear model.
- [ ] Compare the results and decide which model is best suited for predicting revenue based on the provided variables.
